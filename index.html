Drowsiness Detection Study

Camera and Wearable-based Feasibility Study — VITAL Project, EDAG India / THI Germany

1. What this study is about

This is a research study conducted as part of a Master's thesis at Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt (THI) in cooperation with EDAG Engineering Group. The thesis (Project VITAL) develops a real-time drowsiness detection system that combines a wearable-based heart-rate-variability (HRV) model with a camera-based facial-feature model, with the goal of evaluating whether combining these two modalities improves detection reliability compared to either modality alone.

This session collects reference data on how each modality performs on healthy adult subjects during a passive monotonous-driving task. Your data will be used to evaluate the model's per-modality accuracy and, if you consent, to further train the wearable HRV model. The study makes no medical or diagnostic claims about individual participants.

2. What participation involves

You will complete three sessions: morning (M1), after lunch (M2), and end of workday (M3). Each session lasts about 40 minutes, matching the session duration used in the research dataset (UL-DD) that the model was trained on.

During each session you will:

  • Wear a Samsung Galaxy Watch on your non-dominant wrist
  • Sit in front of a laptop with a camera facing you
  • Watch a 40-minute highway driving video
  • Listen for beeps — these are triggered by our HRV and camera models when they detect signs of drowsiness in your data
  • When you hear a beep, click the on-screen brake pedal as fast as possible (this measures how quickly you can react)
  • After each brake response, rate your current sleepiness on the KSS scale shown below the video

Some sessions may have very few beeps or none at all — this simply means the models did not detect drowsiness during that session, which is a valid result.

3. Data we collect

  • Heart rate and inter-beat intervals from the watch
  • Camera-based drowsiness score, blink rate, blink duration, head pose, face detection status
  • Your KSS self-ratings throughout each session
  • Your reaction time (how fast you click brake after each beep)
  • Context: approximate sleep the night before, caffeine intake, meal timing, medications, wellbeing

We do NOT store: your name, employee ID, or any direct identifier alongside the sensor data. You will be assigned a participant ID (e.g., P01) that is used in all data files.

4. Data storage and transfer

Cross-border transfer: Data collected in India will be transferred to and analyzed in Germany at THI / EDAG for the purpose of this research.

Data will be stored until the completion of the associated thesis research and subsequent scientific publication.

Access to your data is limited to the researcher (Shubh Patel, THI/EDAG) and the thesis supervisor (Prof. Schwerd, THI). Data will not be shared with anyone else without your renewed consent.

7. Karolinska Sleepiness Scale (KSS)

You will use this 9-point scale to rate your current sleepiness. The buttons are always visible below the video during the session — click whichever level best matches how you feel at any moment, especially right after each beep.

1Extremely alert
2Very alert
3Alert
4Rather alert
5Neither alert nor sleepy
6Some signs of sleepiness
7Sleepy, but no difficulty remaining awake
8Sleepy, some effort to keep awake
9Very sleepy, great effort to keep awake, fighting sleep

What will happen

  1. A highway driving video will play in the main area of the screen.
  2. Watch the video normally — imagine you are driving.
  3. Our HRV and camera models continuously analyze your data in the background. When either model detects signs of drowsiness in you, it will trigger a short beep.
  4. The moment you hear a beep, click the big BRAKE pedal on the right as fast as you can.
  5. After each brake press, click your current KSS rating (1-9) using the buttons at the bottom of the screen.
  6. You can also click a KSS rating anytime during the session if your state changes.
  7. If no beep occurs during the session, that just means the models did not detect drowsiness. You can still update your KSS rating at any time.
  8. The session runs for 40 minutes.
