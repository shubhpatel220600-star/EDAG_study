Study Information & Privacy Notice

Please review the study details before proceeding.

1. Study Description

This proof-of-concept study investigates how effectively camera-based and wearable-based solutions can detect natural changes in human fatigue throughout the day. This is a purely observational study conducted at a standard desk; there is no vehicle involvement and no driving simulation required.

2. Session Procedure

Participants will complete three separate sessions (Morning, Post-Lunch, Evening) to capture the natural daily drift of fatigue.

  • Duration: 20 to 25 minutes per session.
  • Task: You will sit quietly for a 2-minute resting baseline, followed by a 10-minute standardized screen task. Talking and smartphone usage are strictly prohibited.
  • Sensors: You will wear a Samsung Galaxy Watch on your non-dominant wrist and will be recorded by a standard webcam.

3. Data Protection & GDPR Compliance

Cross-Border Transfer Notice: Data collected during these sessions in India will be securely transferred to servers in Germany for analysis. Under the GDPR, this requires your explicit consent.
  • What is Collected: Physiological wearable data (HR, IBI, Accelerometer), camera-extracted features (fatigue scores, blink rates, head posture), KSS scores, context data, and timestamps.
  • What is NOT Collected: Your name, employee ID, or email will not be stored. All data is pseudonymized using a Participant ID.
  • Purpose: Master's thesis research and algorithm training. Data will never be used for HR evaluations.
  • Subject Rights: You have the right to request access, correction, deletion, or withdraw consent at any time without consequence.

4. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

  • 18+ years old.
  • No serious cardiovascular conditions or pacemakers.
  • No wrist tattoos (interferes with optical sensors).
  • Not taking heart-rate-altering medications.
